As a newbie creator I wanted to have a 3D printer using which I can print 3D objects and work on my creative work.

I searched and tried to gather information on 3D printers to buy, and as an informed person, I wanted to buy a 3D printer that could be:
- Modular and support customization.
- Build size more than 200 mm x 200 mm x 200 mm.
- Affordable.

And the answer to this wish list is **NO**, I cannot get all three things at the same time.
Either I can get a cheaper model with smaller build dimensions, or an expensive model with a larger build size.
Modularity and customization support are common factors in all build sizes.
